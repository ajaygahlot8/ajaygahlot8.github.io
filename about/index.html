Story

I have spent about 11 years in fintech (insurance, payments, lending) and enterprise SaaS. I started as a backend engineer, moved into tech lead roles, and became an Engineering Manager at Gojek in late 2023.

The part of the job I care most about is the one that is easy to lose as a manager: staying close enough to the code to make good calls. At work that means an AI-first engineering practice. At home it means a personal agent stack on Hermes that I can phone on WhatsApp.
